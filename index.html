What to check before ordering

  1. Save the exact variant. Keep the original seller URL, color, size, and measurements in one row. Record when you checked the price.
  2. Review the return window. Check when it starts and who pays return shipping. If your agent provides warehouse QC photos, match the size tag and variant to your order and look for visible damage.
  3. Compare shipping quotes for the same parcel. Use the same destination, packed dimensions, and weight. Ask which weight the route bills on and whether the quote includes insurance or other fees.
  4. Add up the full cost. Combine the item price, seller-to-warehouse delivery, agent fees, international shipping, and applicable taxes or duties. Convert each amount to the same currency before comparing quotes.

iTaoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the two buttons take me?

Both open the CAFANS homepage, where you can browse the catalog. Ordering happens through the seller or shopping agent you choose.

Are the displayed prices shipping-inclusive?

The cards show estimated item prices. Check the original seller listing for the current price, then ask your agent for a separate shipping quote.

What’s the difference between a listing photo and a QC photo?

A listing photo shows what the seller advertises. A warehouse QC photo, when available, shows the item received for your order. Use it to check details such as the color, size tag, and visible condition.
